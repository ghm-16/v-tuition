About Ms. Vila

A scientist's mind, a teacher's heart.

Ms. Vila holds a BSc in Biomedical Sciences and has spent more than 20 years teaching Primary Maths and Science across Singapore. What started as a passion for making science make sense has grown into a tuition practice trusted by families year after year.

She believes every child learns differently. Some need concepts broken into smaller steps; others need the confidence to try a harder question. Her lessons focus on real understanding, not rote memorisation.

Many of her students go on to score their dream grades at PSLE. She's just as proud of watching them stop being afraid of a hard question.

What Ms. Vila Teaches

Subjects & Lesson Formats

Primary Mathematics

Whole numbers, fractions, ratio, algebra and the trickiest model-drawing word problems, built up level by level from P1 to P6.

Primary Science

Diversity, systems, cycles and energy, taught through everyday examples so concepts stick well beyond the exam hall.

PSLE Intensive Prep

Focused, exam-smart revision in the run-up to PSLE: past-year papers, common traps, and a plan built around where your child is now.

In-Person or Online

Home-based lessons islandwide, or live online lessons for families who prefer to learn from home — whichever suits your child best.
